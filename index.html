Our Story

Dentistry Built on Trust & Expertise

Levin Goldman & Ezra Dental Clinic has served the Far Rockaway community for over two decades. Founded on the principle that exceptional dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and compassionate, our clinic has grown into a trusted pillar of the neighborhood.

Our mission is simple: treat every patient like family. We combine cutting-edge dental technology with a gentle, patient-first approach — because we know that great dental health is the foundation of overall wellness.

✓

State-of-the-art equipment — digital X-rays, 3D imaging, and the latest in painless dentistry.

✓

Patient-centered care — unhurried appointments, clear communication, and zero judgment.

✓

Accepting most insurance — including Medicaid, Medicare, and all major dental plans.

✓

Same-day emergency slots — because dental pain shouldn't have to wait.
