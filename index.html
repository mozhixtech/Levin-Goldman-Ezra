Our Story

Dentistry Built on Trust & Expertise

Levin Goldman & Ezra Dental Clinic has served the Far Rockaway community for over two decades. Founded on the principle that exceptional dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and compassionate.

Our mission: treat every patient like family. We combine cutting-edge dental technology with a gentle, patient-first approach — because great dental health is the foundation of overall wellness.

✓

State-of-the-art equipment — digital X-rays, 3D imaging, and the latest in painless dentistry.

✓

Patient-centered care — unhurried appointments, clear communication, zero judgment.

✓

Accepting most insurance — including Medicaid, Medicare, and all major dental plans.

✓

Same-day emergency slots — dental pain shouldn't have to wait.
